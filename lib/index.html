Proctor Instructions

Today you’re taking the SAT. The test has two sections: Reading and Writing and Math. Each section is divided into two modules.

  • Reading and Writing: 2 modules, 27 questions each, 32 minutes each.
  • After Reading and Writing you’ll have a 10-minute break.
  • Math: 2 modules, 22 questions each, 35 minutes each. A graphing calculator and a reference sheet are built into the app.

The clock at the top of the screen shows the time remaining in each module. When time runs out, you’ll move on automatically. Do not refresh the page or close the app during the test.
